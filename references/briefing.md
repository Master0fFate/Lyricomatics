# Briefing

Interview like a producer with ten minutes, not a intake form with twelve blanks.

Ask **3–6** questions. Pick the ones that would change the draft. Sit on the rest.

Skip the interview when the user already gave **pocket + pressure/purpose + voice + forbidden**. Era and melody status become required only when they materially change the form or fit. CAST and write.

If they **explicitly** say just write it / no questions, ask nothing. Silent CAST. See [room-work.md](room-work.md).

A title and a mood is **not** a complete brief and **not** just-write-it. Interview.

## How to pick

Missing pocket → genre / form first. If they already named a microgenre the router cannot cover, do not keep interviewing about "what genre." Finish pressure/voice, then [niche-research.md](niche-research.md).
Missing blood → event and chorus job.
Missing mouth → who sings, who hears, banned words.
Missing texture → one behavior, object, verbal habit, or unfair image. A proper noun is optional.
Named historical lane → ask era or reference period if it is unclear.
Lyrics-to-existing-music → ask for melody/demo, bar map, scansion, or phrase slots.
Vague on everything → start with the five bite questions, then at most one more.

Never paste the banks below into chat. Never paste or number the full bank at a human.

After answers, do not recap as a corporate brief. CAST in 5–10 lines, build the HOOK, and CUT — or ask the one follow-up that is still load-bearing.

## Bite first (prefer these)

- If this song were one text message, what is it?
- What happened — event, not mood?
- What must we not resolve?
- What object would be in the video that isn’t a cliché prop?
- Which line should hurt to sing?
- What is still true when the narrator is wrong?

## Universal bank (menu, not a script)

Steal only what the user did not already answer.

1. Who sings, and who are they to the listener — lover, threat, priest, peer, persona?
2. What is happening. Scene and verb, not weather-as-feelings.
3. What must the chorus **do** — plead, flex, confess, command, mourn, seduce?
4. Which words or phrases are out of voice. Ban them.
5. Form: radio pop, story song, mantra, AABA, strophic, verses-and-hook, want-song, other.
6. Era target: period-authentic, contemporary, or a named blend.
7. Melody status: free lyric, topline to existing music, or revision against a demo/scansion.
8. Rhyme appetite: dense, moderate, sparse.
9. True names, jobs, places, verbal habits, or behaviors worth locking.
10. One unfair-advantage detail — what only this narrator would notice or admit.
11. Ending neatness: scab (0) to sermon (5). Default scab-ward if they shrug.
12. What would make a skeptical A&R mutter “machine.” Avoid that residue.
13. Explicit content and ethical hard lines.
14. Attitude anchors: name two songs as *feel*, never as lines to lift.

Never paste this expanded bank as a questionnaire. It is a menu, not paperwork invented by a minor bureaucratic god.

## Genre already named

Do **not** expand the universal list. Read `genres/<slug>.md` and take **one or two** of its briefing questions instead of inventing a second interview.

Unknown genre: ask pocket and chorus job before any other craft.

## Already-complete brief

Treat as complete when you can fill, without guessing:

- pocket (genre or a named hybrid)
- pressure/purpose (what the song is doing in the room; plot is optional for mantra, praise, dance, and novelty)
- voice (who, to whom, diction/bans)
- forbidden (stated words, moves, ethical lines; “none” is a valid answer)

Also require era when period form/diction matters, and melody data when the user expects exact fit. Otherwise those may stay open and singability stays provisional.

Then CAST. No “quick clarifying questions” out of habit.

## Anti-patterns

- Dumping the full question bank.
- Asking mood when you needed an event.
- Asking for reference tracks and then quoting them.
- Interviewing after “just write it.”
- Softening a biting question into workshop-speak.
