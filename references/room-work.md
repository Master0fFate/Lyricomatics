# ROOM WORK

A recording-room collaboration. Not a numbered QC gate. Not a rubric you announce.

Six stages. Skip the interview only when they **explicitly** said just write it / no questions. Title plus mood is an incomplete brief — LISTEN, do not skip. Do not skip CAST or OFFER. Skip CUT on **critique** jobs. SING/BLEED still happen on whatever is on the page.

## LISTEN

Get the brief. Do not draft on vibes.

**Do:** If they did **not** explicitly say just-write-it, and pocket, stakes, voice, or forbidden is missing, read [briefing.md](briefing.md) and ask 3–6 questions that would change the ink. Then stop. Title + mood is missing, not permission to skip.

**Good:** The next draft is constrained. You know who is speaking, what happened, what the chorus must *do*, and what is banned.

**Fail:** Questionnaire dump. Mood instead of event. Interviewing a complete brief. “Any reference tracks?” as a stall. Drafting while you still don’t know the pocket.

## CAST

Decide the record before you write it.

Name, in 5–10 lines if anything was inferred:

- narrator and addressee
- POV and tense
- genre pocket and form (family slug, plus niche card if [niche-research.md](niche-research.md) fired)
- **one** governing image system
- title candidates

**Good:** A committed camera. One metaphor domain. Titles that could be said out loud. Form that matches the pocket (mantra stays mantra; want-song wants). Niche named: you learned the mouth before you faked it.

**Fail (niche):** Browsing a known family to look busy. Quoting a hot song as research. Skipping the family kill list because a blog had a vibe.

**Fail:** Discovering POV in verse 3. Genre soup. Three image systems. A two-page treatment. Asking permission to have taste.

### Silent CAST

User **explicitly** says **just write it** or **no questions**:

- Ask nothing.
- If they already named a niche the router cannot cover, [niche-research.md](niche-research.md) may still run. That is not an interview.
- Still decide every CAST item. Do it on your side of the glass.
- Do not print a 10-line cast unless a single inferred choice is load-bearing (wrong pocket would wreck the song). Then: three lines, max, and the draft immediately.
- Still OFFER when you hand it over.

A title and a mood with no “just write it” is **not** silent CAST. That is LISTEN.

Silent is not sloppy. Silent is refusing to hold the session hostage after they ordered speed.

## CUT

Draft and rewrite jobs only. Critique jobs skip this stage.

Write as if tracking tomorrow.

Genre-true. Specific. One committed draft, not a flight of options.
Do not invent grief, an empty chair, or a bill because a celebration “needs cost.” If they asked for praise, a party, a joke, or clean triumph, write that.

Load per SKILL.md draft-read budget (genre + anti-slop, or craft if they pasted lyrics with no pocket).

**Good:** Proper nouns or time crumbs when the pocket wants a room. Verses prove, chorus claims — unless the pocket is mantra/vamp. V2 is not V1 with the nouns swapped.

**Fail:** Abstract stacks. Therapy three-act. Counterfeit melancholy on a joy brief. Two full songs and “which vibe.” Writing the metaphor and then explaining it.

## SING

Speak it if you can. If you cannot, mark stresses and breath groups on the page. See [rhyme-prosody.md](rhyme-prosody.md).

Mouth-feel. Hook a tired human can catch on pass one. Singability without a supplied melody is provisional.

Open rhyme-prosody only if the mouth fails.

**Good:** Chorus syllables stable enough to recognize (mantra pockets may loop). Speech order, then rhyme — not rhyme, then Yoda. Hook is shoutable or croonable in the actual pocket.

**Fail:** Page-only cleverness as the only test. Consonant clusters on a belted title. Inverted syntax for a perfect end-rhyme. Treating intentional syncopation as an error.

## BLEED

Forensic pass. You are looking for residue that smells like a caption.

Knife: [anti-slop.md](anti-slop.md). If they asked for notes, not a rewrite: [critique.md](critique.md).

Kill: abstract-noun piles, motivational-poster diction, symmetrical healing arcs, missing idiosyncrasy, inverted syntax, title-stuffing, sermon endings.

**Good:** Fewer abstracts. Broken verse symmetry. One unresolved speck left on purpose. User’s diction intact — you did not “upgrade” them into essay English.

**Fail:** Polish that adds Latinate. Killing the weird line because it was uneven. Moral sticker on the last bar. A second rewrite that sanded off the voice you were hired to keep.

## OFFER

Hand them a sheet, not a TED talk.

Conventions: [delivery.md](delivery.md). Comma-run stanza layout is law — copy the shape in SKILL.md Output. Not one clause per row.

**Good:** Labeled lyric. Optional one-line scheme note. Optional short alt list. **2–4** questions that would change the next pass. Then you shut up.

**Fail:** Essay wrapping (“this song explores…”). Dump and vanish. Twelve rhetorical questions. Three complete alternate songs. Pep talk. Apologizing for taste.

## Passing through

LISTEN may be zero questions only on a complete brief or an explicit just-write-it. CAST may be silent on just-write-it. Critique skips CUT. On a hot draft, SING/BLEED still happen in the same pass — but they happen. If you cannot point to a hook that works in the pocket and a slop cut you considered, you did not finish the room.
