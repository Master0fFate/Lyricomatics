# Electronic

Lyric real estate is scarce. House wants a sentence that can loop without rotting. Trip-hop wants a crooked image and negative space. Do not novelize a club vocal.

## Sub-lanes

- **House / peak EDM vocal** — 1–2 hooks, maybe a short verse. Repetition is the arrangement. Long notes, open vowels, DJ-loopable 4–8 bar phrases. Yearning or release, not a plot.
- **Afterhours** — still mantra, cooler blood, less festival grammar. Same scarcity of unique lines.
- **Trip-hop / downtempo** — denser poetic fragments allowed, lower voice, incomplete sentences, noir that is mundane not costume. No tidy resolution.

If they want a full radio pop novel on a four-on-the-floor, that is `pop.md` — and you should still steal this kill list so the verse does not eat the loop.

## Form

House/EDM: minimal unique lyric. One sentence of yearning or release, maybe a pre-gesture, maybe a two-line verse that exists to make the mantra hit. Six unique dense sections is malpractice.

Trip-hop: fragments, return, residue. Sections may not have pop names. A “chorus” might be a crooked image you revisit changed. Do not fake a bridge of wisdom.

Repetition is not laziness. If you are bored repeating the line, the line is weak or you are in the wrong pocket.

Do not explain the mantra. Explanation is how club vocals become sermons.

## Rhyme pressure

House: low. Mantra. Identical hits can be the point. Cute scheme on a peak-time vocal sounds like a nursery in a warehouse.

Trip-hop: low to medium. Image chains, family rhyme, slant. Do not rap-rhyme a downtempo fragment unless CAST named a spoken lane.

Never invert. These mouths are either held vowels or late-night talk.

## Pocket / mouth

House: long notes, open vowels (*ah, oh, you, away, open, body* only if earned). Loopable phrases. Consonant clusters die on the hold. Write less.

Trip-hop: behind the beat, incomplete sentences okay. Leave negative space. Do not fill the bar because you fear silence.

If you cannot imagine the same line lasting forty cycles without rotting, rewrite the line, do not write forty variations.

Speak-test: house should be easy at volume; trip-hop should be easy at 2 a.m. in a small room.

## POV & voice

House often second-person euphoria or self-dissolve. “You” as floor, lover, or chemical — pick, do not blur into cosmic brochure. Afterhours can be lonelier without becoming a therapy session.

Trip-hop: interior monologue. Mundane concrete — kitchen fluorescent, receipt, bus window — beats cigarette-and-rain costume.

Festival-brochure transcendence is the enemy. “Take me higher / feel the love” is not a personality.

No rave spirituality essay. No trip-hop adjective fog (velvet, neon, infinite dusk) without one object motif that changes state.

## Hook

House: one sentence of yearning or release. That is the song. Surround it with almost nothing.

Trip-hop: crooked image as title. Do not tidy it.

Do not write a post-chorus that is a second thesis. Stamps, chops, fragments — yes. New philosophy — no.

## Kill list

- “Take me higher”
- “Feel the love tonight”
- Festival-brochure transcendence
- Trip-hop cigarette-and-rain costume
- Six unique dense sections on a peak-time brief
- Explaining the mantra
- Trip-hop with a tidy resolution
- Abstract rave spirituality
- Full pop novel for a vocal-chop song
- Adjective fog with no object

## AI failure modes

Writing a singer-songwriter album into a loop. Poster euphoria. Random sad adjectives as trip-hop. Every section a new paragraph. Closed vowels on long notes. Moral ending. Cosmic you with no body. Costume noir. Mantra that rots because it was clever instead of true.

If the unique-word count could fund a folk EP, you overwrote.

## Briefing questions

1. Loop mantra or narrative verse?
2. Peak-time, afterhours, or downtempo / trip-hop?
3. Which one image can repeat forty times without rotting?

## Hybrid note

Primary when scarcity, loop, or fragment-noir lead. Dance-pop with a real verse/pre/chorus engine: `pop.md` primary; this kill list so you do not write brochure transcendence. Folktronica: `folk.md` primary for shame and ending; this file’s no-tidy-resolution and no-novel-on-a-loop rules. Rap on a club bed: `hip-hop.md` for the verse; this file owns the hook’s scarcity. House-gospel: `gospel.md` if testimony/choir/vamp lead; do not turn a praise name into “take me higher.” Punk-electronic: `punk.md` if slogan-shout leads; steal this scarcity so you do not essay over a loop.
