# R&B

Heat with logistics. Desire that has a toothbrush in the sink. The hook is a caress or an accusation, not a mood board.

## Sub-lanes

- **R&B** — verse/chorus body; intimate you; air for runs and behind-the-beat talk.
- **Soul** — testimony of the body and the bill; plain speech that can be wrung; gospel cousin without stealing church form unless briefed.
- **Neo-soul** — conversational enjambment, circular vamps, spoken asides, through-composed verses that still close a feeling.

Rap-sung lanes: if bars are the product, `hip-hop.md` is primary. If the run and the ache are the product, stay here.

## Form

Verse–chorus with long pockets. Bridge as confession or flip — a fact that recasts the hook — not a bigger adjective.

Neo-soul may wander the verse and live on a vamp. Wandering is not a paragraph of advice. Circular does not mean static: the object in the room should change state.

Do not overwrite. The singer will occupy the holes. A page that looks sparse can be the honest draft.

Second verse should shift power, time of night, or who still has keys. Forgiveness arcs that graduate on schedule are fake.

## Rhyme pressure

Low to medium. Groove and vowel color beat dense schemes. Soft internals are welcome. Punchline multis feel try-hard unless the artist’s lane is rap-sung and the brief said so.

Do not over-rhyme smooth music. Perfect couplets in a bedroom voice sound like a greeting card that went to school.

End on open, singable words in the hook. Closed clusters belong in talky asides, not on the title hold.

## Pocket / mouth

Write less than you think. Leave space for runs, holds, behind the beat. If every beat has a syllable, you stole the singer’s job.

Neo-soul loves speech that breaks across the bar. Let the sentence lean. Do not “pretty” the ugly stress if the ugly stress is how this person talks.

Hook vowels should be harmony-friendly — ah, oh, ee that can be stacked without chewing.

Read it slow, as if the track already exists and you are late into the pocket. If you are panting, cut.

## POV & voice

Intimate second person. First-person desire, regret, stalemate. “We” for a couple mythos only when the myth has a cost.

God / body / bed triangle is traditional. Handle with apartment detail, not incense smoke. Body did something; heart is not a witness you depose.

Essay words — reciprocity, boundaries, toxicity as a diagnosis — do not survive unless this narrator would say them at 1 a.m. Usually they would not. Show the behavior.

Perfect grammar is a tell. Fragments, swallows, repeated names: human. Caricature dialect: banned.

## Hook

Emotional plain speech you could ad-lib around. Title as caress or accusation. Pick.

The hook should survive being almost spoken. If it only works as a poem on a screen, it is not this lane.

Do not build a chorus that could be pinned under a sunset. One claim, one body fact, one cost.

## Kill list

- Silk sheets as default set
- Generic candlelight
- “You’re my everything”
- Weather-as-feelings with no apartment
- “Toxic” as a personality sticker, no behavior
- Relationship-advice nouns instead of scenes
- Pinterest-board chorus
- Over-rhyme on a smooth pocket
- Porny vagueness *or* pure worship with no human mess
- Baby→Jesus noun swap (that is a gospel ethics fail, not a holy upgrade)

## AI failure modes

Bedroom essay. Perfect symmetrical forgiveness. Undigested therapy lexicon. Worship without a body, or body without a person. Every section equally wet with adjectives. No keys, no second pillow, no read receipt, no leftover food. Narrator who sounds like a consent workshop and a perfume ad at once.

If you cannot name what their body did last time, you wrote a vibe.

## Briefing questions

1. What did their body do last time — not what did their heart feel?
2. Is this seduction, aftermath, or stalemate?
3. Which everyday object is still in the room?

## Hybrid note

Stay primary when intimacy and run-space outrank bar density or radio-stamp duty. Pop-R&B: keep this air and object-logistics; steal pop’s title discipline; run `pop.md` kill list so you do not own-the-night. Rap feature: guest verse obeys `hip-hop.md`; the hook stays here. Gospel-R&B: if the addressee is God or the vamp is the song, `gospel.md` is primary. Trap-soul: this file’s mouth; hip-hop kill list on the flex so throne-talk does not crash the bedroom.
