# Rock

A chorus a band could print on a shirt. Verses like rooms and arguments. Not a pit anthem — that is punk. Not a bedroom diary with no throat — that is folk.

## Sub-lanes

- **Rock** — verse/chorus, downbeat stress, chantable claim, fist-sized title. Enemy optional but if rebellion is claimed, name the rule.
- **Indie** — elliptical okay, cold open okay, anti-chorus okay, one-line mantra okay. Strange concrete image over scheme. Do not explain the symbol.

Garage that wants slogans and spit belongs in `punk.md`. Arena feelings with no object still fail here.

## Form

Rock: verse–chorus, title hook that can be yelled without a lyric sheet. Pre if it lifts; skip if it only stalls. Bridge as invert or reveal, not a louder restatement.

Indie: form may refuse the handshake. Cold open on an image. Chorus that is one stubborn line. Through-build without a traditional hook — allowed if the residue image does the branding. Do not use “indie” as permission to wander without a spine.

V2 changes the room’s power or the time of night. Identical architecture with new adjectives is radio-rock slop even in indie clothes.

Punk-length patience does not apply; this lane can bear a second verse. It cannot bear a second verse that is a photocopy.

## Rhyme pressure

Rock: medium. Ends on the hook should catch. Verses can slant.

Indie: low to medium. Image outranks scheme. Cute perfect rhyme that winks too hard breaks the spell. Do not compensate for thin rhyme by thickening adjectives.

Never invert a line into unspeakable order to land *fire* on the downbeat. Write the claim in speech, then find the musical stress.

## Pocket / mouth

Rock choruses want fist-pump stress on the downbeats. Title words on the hits. Open vowels for the shout-along.

Indie can talk-sing. Ugly stresses that sound like a person in a kitchen beat pretty stresses that sound like a caption.

Consonant sludge on a belted chorus will fail in the room. Save clusters for talky verse asides.

If the chorus needs more than one breath for a single slogan-line, it is probably two lines pretending to be one.

## POV & voice

Alienation in first person is common; it still needs a room. Accusatory second person needs a target with a noun. Indie third-person vignettes work when the camera is cruel and specific.

Collective “we” for a band-as-gang is fine when the gang is in the brief. Generic youth-we is a campaign.

Thesaurus emo is banned. Small verbs. Objects that recur with changed state — a jacket, a key, a bus route — not a nebula of despair.

Rebellion without a target is karaoke. Name the boss, algorithm, landlord, church board, or shut up about the system.

## Hook

Rock: chantable claim. Could survive a shirt, a shout, a last-call room.

Indie: strange concrete that doubles as title. Leave one image unexplained on purpose. If you annotate it, you killed it.

Do not write “rise up” unless someone specific is standing on someone specific.

## Kill list

- Broken glass / empty bottle as default furniture
- Screaming at the void
- Rain on the window as personality
- Rebellion with no target
- Thesaurus emo (“nebula of my despair”)
- Indie that explains its own symbolism
- Identical “rise up” choruses
- Aesthetic adjective fog (neon, velvet, infinite, broken light)
- Teenage doom with no address
- Therapy nouns in leather

## AI failure modes

Generic stadium morality. Every rock chorus interchangeable. Indie as a pile of tasteful adjectives and no object. Verses that describe a mood board instead of an argument. Soft-focus alienation. No proper noun. Bridge that is the chorus with bigger weather. Cost-free triumph or cost-free doom — both are posters.

If you can swap the band name and nothing cracks, keep cutting.

## Briefing questions

1. Arena throat, club throat, or bedroom-indie throat?
2. Who is the enemy, if any — named, not “the system”?
3. Which one image must stay unexplained?

## Hybrid note

Primary when a band throat and a shirt-print or residue-image hook matter more than pit-shout duration or kitchen-diary shame. Rock/punk border: if the chorus must be yelled in one fist of breath and the verse is indictment, `punk.md` owns it. Indie/folk border: if the shame is diaristic and the throat is solo, `folk.md`. Indie/electronic: keep this image rule; run `electronic.md` kill list so you do not novelize a loop. Rap-rock: pick who owns the grid — if bars, `hip-hop.md`; if chant, stay here and steal hip-hop’s kill list for throne-talk.
